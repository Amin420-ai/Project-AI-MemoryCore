## Shift handover / Serahan syif (5 min)
- [ ] Outgoing staff tells: queue, low stock, equipment problems, complaints, special orders. / Staf keluar maklum: beratur, stok rendah, masalah peralatan, aduan, tempahan khas.
- [ ] Incoming staff checks cash sheet, temp log, cleaned stations. / Staf masuk semak helaian tunai, log suhu, stesen bersih.
- [ ] Write in handover book: `[tarikh | masa | dari → ke | nota]`. / Tulis dalam buku serahan.
