# 06 — Cash & POS / Tunai & POS

**Who / Siapa:** Cashier on shift + supervisor check / Juruwang + semakan penyelia

## Opening float / Modal pembukaan
- [ ] Count float: RM`[jumlah]` (notes + coins); write in cash sheet. / Kira modal; catat.
- [ ] Open POS; log in with own PIN (never share). / Buka POS; log masuk dengan PIN sendiri.

## During shift / Semasa syif
1. Count money back to customer aloud. / Kira baki kepada pelanggan.
2. Keep notes under tray until change given. / Simpan wang di bawah dulang sehingga baki diberi.
3. Cash drawer closed except during a sale. / Laci tutup kecuali semasa jualan.
4. Move cash above RM`[had]` to safe `[tempat]` (cash drop). / Pindah tunai berlebihan ke peti.
5. Void/refund only with supervisor approval; write reason. / Void/bayar balik dengan kelulusan penyelia.
6. E-wallet/QR: confirm payment received on the merchant app before handing food. / Sahkan pembayaran dalam aplikasi sebelum hidang.

## Closing cash-up / Kira tunai penutupan
- [ ] Print POS daily report (X/Z). / Cetak laporan harian POS.
- [ ] Count cash; expected = float + cash sales − refunds. / Kira tunai; jangkaan = modal + jualan tunai − bayar balik.
- [ ] Compare: over/short → write amount and likely reason. / Banding: lebih/kurang → catat jumlah dan sebab.
- [ ] Short/over more than RM`[x]` → supervisor recounts; tell Pkin. / Beza lebih RM`[x]` → penyelia kira semula; maklum Pkin.
- [ ] Put tomorrow's float aside; bank deposit `[bila/di mana]`. / Asingkan modal esok; deposit bank.
- [ ] Sign the cash sheet (cashier + supervisor). / Tandatangan helaian tunai.

## Daily record / Rekod harian
`[tarikh | jualan POS | tunai | QR/kad | bayar balik | beza | tandatangan]`
