# 09 — Incident & Post-Mortem / Insiden & Pembelajaran

**When / Bila:** accident, injury, customer illness, pest sighting, cash loss, equipment failure, serious complaint.

## Immediate response / Tindakan segera
1. Safety first — help the person; call 999 if serious. / Keselamatan dahulu; hubungi 999 jika serius.
2. Stop the cause (turn off gas/equipment; isolate suspect food — keep a sample, do not throw). / Hentikan punca; asingkan makanan disyaki, simpan sampel.
3. Tell supervisor, then Pkin. / Beritahu penyelia, kemudian Pkin.
4. Customer suspected food poisoning: note name, phone, what eaten, time; advise medical help; do not argue about cause. / Disyaki keracunan makanan: catat nama, telefon, makanan, masa; nasihat rawatan perubatan.
5. Authority contact / Pihak berkuasa: PKD `[no.]`, PBT `[no.]`.

## Rules / Peraturan
- No blame; focus on the process. / Tiada salahkan; fokus proses.
- Append-only: do not rewrite old entries. / Hanya tambah; jangan ubah entri lama.
- Review entries monthly with the team; update the SOP that failed. / Semak bulanan; kemas kini SOP berkaitan.
