# 04 — Food Safety & Hygiene / Keselamatan Makanan & Kebersihan

**Who / Siapa:** Everyone, always / Semua staf, sentiasa

## Staff requirements / Syarat staf
- [ ] Every food handler has completed an approved food-handler course and typhoid vaccination (reported requirement under Food Hygiene Regulations 2009 — see README). / Setiap pengendali makanan telah tamat kursus pengendali makanan diiktiraf dan suntikan tifoid (rujuk README).
- [ ] Keep certificate copies in `[lokasi fail]`. Record expiry `[tarikh]`. / Simpan salinan sijil.
- [ ] Sick with vomiting, diarrhoea, fever, or infected wound → do NOT handle food; tell supervisor. / Sakit muntah, cirit-birit, demam, luka berjangkit → JANGAN kendali makanan; beritahu penyelia.

## Handwashing — 20 seconds / Basuh tangan
Wash: start of shift, after toilet, after touching raw meat/eggs, money, phone, rubbish, face/hair, eating, cleaning. / Cuci: mula syif, selepas tandas, daging/telur mentah, wang, telefon, sampah, muka/rambut, makan, membersih.
Steps: wet → soap → rub palms, backs, fingers, thumbs, nails → rinse → dry with paper towel. / Basah → sabun → gosok → bilas → lap kertas.

## Temperature control / Kawalan suhu
Keep food out of 5°C–60°C ("danger zone"; confirm limits with PKD — see README). / Jauhkan makanan dari 5°C–60°C (sahkan dengan PKD).
| Item | Target | Check |
|------|--------|-------|
| Fridge / Peti sejuk | ≤ 5°C | open + close + `[tengah hari]` |
| Freezer / Peti beku | ≤ -18°C `[sahkan]` | open + close |
| Hot-held food | ≥ 60°C | every 2 h |
| Cooked/reheated food | `[isi: ≥ 75°C]` core `[sahkan dengan PKD]` | each batch |

Temperature log / Log suhu: `[tarikh | masa | peralatan | suhu | tindakan | inisial]`

## Storage / Penyimpanan
- Raw below cooked in fridge; covered; labelled with date. / Mentah di bawah, masak di atas; tutup; label tarikh.
- FIFO — first in, first out. / Masuk dulu, keluar dulu.
- Chemicals away from food, in labelled containers. / Bahan kimia jauh dari makanan, berlabel.
- Nothing on floor; keep ≥15 cm off. / Tiada di lantai.
- Separate boards/knives: raw vs ready-to-eat. / Papan/pisau berasingan.

## Cleaning schedule / Jadual pembersihan
| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| Every use | Wipe + sanitise surfaces, boards, tongs |
| Daily | Mop floors, empty bins, clean sinks, toilets |
| Weekly | Clean fridges, shelves, drains, vents `[hari]` |
| Monthly | Deep clean, check pest-control `[tarikh]` |

## Pest control / Kawalan perosak
- Keep doors closed, bins covered, no standing water, seal gaps. / Pintu tutup, tong bertutup, tiada air bertakung.
- Use licensed pest-control company: `[syarikat]`; visits `[kekerapan]`. / Syarikat kawalan perosak berlesen.
- Any sign → remove affected food, tell supervisor, SOP 09. / Sebarang tanda → buang, beritahu penyelia, SOP 09.

## Licence & inspection / Lesen & pemeriksaan
- [ ] Business licence from council: `[no. / tarikh luput]`. / Lesen premis PBT.
- [ ] Display licence and hygiene grade `[gred]` where visible. / Paparkan lesen dan gred.
- [ ] Ask your PBT for its inspection checklist; run it monthly. / Minta senarai semak PBT; semak setiap bulan.
